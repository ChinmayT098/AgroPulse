💡 Key Agronomic Insights Discovered from the Dataset

Findings derived from the loaded observations

1. Moisture-Driven Crops

High-biomass crops such as Sugarcane and Rice require precipitation between 900–1800mm, with yield declining sharply when drought stress occurs.

2. Soil pH Sweet Spot

Optimal nutrient absorption occurs in neutral to slightly alkaline soils (pH 6.2 - 7.5). Extreme acidic or alkaline conditions impede nutrient uptake.

3. Soil Nitrogen & Fertilizer Impact

Nitrogen levels show a strong positive correlation with cereal yields (Wheat, Maize, Rice), confirming the critical role of balanced fertilization.
